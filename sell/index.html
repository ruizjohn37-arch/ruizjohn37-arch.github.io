Questions

Will you pay full market price?

Usually not. A cash, as-is sale saves you repairs, commissions, showings and months of waiting, so offers are below what a fully fixed-up house would list for. If listing with an agent gets you more and you don't mind the work, that may be the better choice, and I'll tell you so.

Do I need to clean or fix anything?

No. Sell it as it sits. Take what you want and leave the rest.

Are there fees?

No agent commissions. Closing costs are spelled out in the contract before you sign anything.

Do I have to accept?

No. An offer is just an offer. If it doesn't work for you, say no and that's the end of it.
